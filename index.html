Questions, answered.

How do I use it?

Plug it into a wall socket and switch the socket on. There is nothing to assemble or install.

Where can I buy it?

On Amazon, Flipkart and Meesho. Choose your design above, then tap the store you prefer; you order and pay there, the way you usually shop.

How does delivery work?

The store you order from delivers it. Its listing shows the delivery date and any charges for your pin code before you pay, and you can track the order in your account there.

Can I return or exchange it?

Returns and exchanges follow the policy of the store you bought from. You will find it on the product listing and in your order details.

How do I choose a design?

Tap any design under "Choose your design". Every lamp on this page, and the lamp in each room photo, changes to show it, so you can see how it looks and glows before you buy.

Can I get a custom design?

Yes. If you would like a design of your own on the lamp, call or WhatsApp Aashi Enterprises on +91 87003 00836 and tell us what you have in mind.

Can I order in bulk?

Yes. For gifting, events or temples, call or WhatsApp Aashi Enterprises on +91 87003 00836 with the designs and quantities you need.

Where does it look best?

Anywhere you want a calm, warm light: beside the bed, in the puja room, a meditation corner, a hallway or your office.

Is it a good gift?

Yes. It is a thoughtful spiritual gift for Diwali, housewarmings, birthdays and elders in the family.

How do I keep it clean?

Unplug it first, then wipe the front gently with a soft dry cloth.
