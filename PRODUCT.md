## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: home devotees in India buying for their own home. They want a small devotional light for the puja room, the bedroom, a meditation corner, the living room or an office desk, something that keeps a sacred symbol softly lit through the night.

Gift buying (Diwali, housewarmings, family) is a real secondary use named in the product description, but it is not the primary audience.

## Product Purpose

The OM Night Lamp is a compact plug-in night lamp with a printed, backlit OM artwork. It brings a calm, warm, devotional glow into a room without any installation.

The website exists to present the lamp clearly and beautifully, build enough trust and desire to buy, and send visitors to a marketplace listing to purchase. Success means visitors understand what the lamp is and how it is used, and click through to buy.

## Positioning

A night light that is also a devotional object: the sacred OM artwork is the lit face of the lamp itself, not a sticker on a generic light. It plugs straight into a wall socket, so the symbol can glow in any corner of the home with no setup.

## Operating Context

- Used at home, mostly at night: puja rooms, bedsides, meditation corners, living rooms, offices, temples.
- Plugs directly into a standard Indian wall socket (two round pins on the back). Use is: plug in, switch the socket on.
- Bought online through marketplaces, not on this website.

## Product Principles

1. The sacred symbol leads. The OM artwork is the hero of every presentation of the product; treat it with reverence.
2. Light is the story. The product's value is a soft, warm, peaceful glow; show it rather than describe it.
3. Honest and simple. Only state facts the seller has confirmed; no invented specs, reviews or urgency.
4. One clear next step. Every path ends at a marketplace listing where the visitor can buy.
5. Built to grow into a series. Structure should accommodate more devotional designs without a rebuild.
